# Fairy Forest Healing: clue cards

Fill in the hiding spots with Liza. Print, cut, hand her card 1 to start.
Each card points to the NEXT station. Card 5 is read after the rainbow.

## Opening (read aloud)

Dark energy has crept into the Fairy Forest and the glow has gone out.
Your wand still holds fairy light. Find the five forest places, wave your
wand, and heal them one by one. Here is your first clue...

## Card 1 → Station 1
Where ______ [spot 1] sits so still,
A patch of forest, cold and ill.
Wave your wand and watch it gleam,
Then find the next with fairy beam.

## Card 2 → Station 2
The forest thanks you, brave and true,
But ______ [spot 2] needs healing too.
Look where ______ [hint about spot 2],
Then swing your wand and light the view.

## Card 3 → Station 3
Two are healed, three more to go,
The darkest place is ______ [spot 3], you know.
Creep up quiet, hold wand high,
And give the shadows one last sigh.

## Card 4 → Station 4
Halfway home, the fairies cheer,
But ______ [spot 4] still hides fear.
Bring your light where ______ [hint],
And chase the gloom away from here.

## Card 5 → Station 5 (final)
One last place the darkness keeps,
Where ______ [spot 5] silently sleeps.
Heal it now with all your might,
And fill the forest full of light!

## Ending (read after the rainbow)
The Fairy Forest is healed! Every tree and flower glows again because of
you. The fairies name you Guardian of the Forest. (Reward: ______ )
