## Distinguish three tests

- ☐ **Preview:** clicks or keys 1–3 advance the matching landmarks. This tests software only.
- ☐ **Button A:** each physical board advances its matching target. This tests its ID and hub connection, not IR reception.
- ☐ **Actual wand:** each board advances the matching target from a real wave. This tests the full input path.
- ☐ Three slow waves at sensor 1 produce three charge increments, not one held input.
- ☐ A wrong landmark gives a gentle clue. In the trail puzzle, it restarts only that three-step pattern.
- ☐ Training can be replayed; leaving it preserves story progress and the campaign spellbook.
